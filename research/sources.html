What the cross-check adds. Twelve consumer-commission decisions on OYO check-in denials were found and verified from orders or full press reports. In every reported decision on the merits that was found, OYO's "only a booking platform" defence was rejected. The hotel reasons recorded by the commissions (contract ended or "no association with OYO", a payment dispute with OYO, extra money demanded, full, no record) are the same four or five causes as in the app reviews. On the complaint forum, 17 of 40 complaints involve a payment or contract dispute between OYO and the hotel that lands on the guest, 15 of 40 guests were asked for more money at the desk, and OYO offered another hotel in only 4. The limits: court cases and forum posts are the extreme tail, most incidents are from 2018 to 2023, and none of this measures how often denials happen. The full notes are in raw/crosscheck_raw.md.

F. What was searched for and not found

  • Any current or past OYO "check-in guarantee", "free stay" promise or compensation beyond the booking amount. None was found; the only time-bound promise is "Service On-Time", limited to selected hotels in six cities.
  • An absolute number of bookings or room nights for India. Not disclosed; the impact model derives bookings from GBV and an assumed booking value.
  • OYO's current commission or revenue-share percentage in India. Not disclosed (the only figure, "roughly 20%", is from 2019 and is not used).
  • The split between prepaid and pay-at-hotel bookings. Not disclosed.
  • OYO's penalty schedule for denials ("OYO Policies", linked from the partner terms). The linked domain did not resolve.
  • Any order or notice from the Central Consumer Protection Authority naming OYO, and any National Consumer Helpline count specific to OYO.
  • FY26 full-year India storefronts and India GBV.
